# Delivery Policy
Delivery slots run from 7am to 10pm every day, including weekends and public holidays.
Customers choose a one-hour slot at checkout; slots fill up quickly on weekend mornings.

Delivery is free on orders above Rs 999. Orders below Rs 999 carry a standard
delivery fee shown at checkout.

Express delivery brings your order within 90 minutes for a fee of Rs 49. Express
delivery is available only in serviceable pin codes and only for in-stock items.

If a delivery fails because the customer is unavailable, the delivery is re-attempted
once on the same day. After a second failed attempt the order is returned to the
store and cancelled. Riders will call the registered phone number before arriving.

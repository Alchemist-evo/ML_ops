# CartVista Plus Membership
CartVista Plus costs Rs 299 per quarter, billed at the start of each quarter.

Plus members get free express delivery on every order, with no minimum order value.
Members also earn 2% cashback on every order, credited to the CartVista wallet within
48 hours of delivery. Cashback can be used on any future order.

You can cancel your Plus membership anytime from the Account page. After cancellation
your benefits continue to run until the end of the current paid period; no partial
refunds are issued for the unused portion of a quarter.

Plus membership is linked to a single account and cannot be shared or transferred.

# Payments Policy
CartVista accepts UPI, credit cards, debit cards and Cash on Delivery (COD).

Cash on Delivery is available only for orders below Rs 5000. Orders of Rs 5000 or more
must be paid online by UPI or card.

COD is unavailable for express orders; express delivery must be prepaid.

All online payments are processed through a secure payment gateway. CartVista never
asks for your card PIN or UPI PIN over the phone. If a payment fails but money is
debited, it is automatically reversed by your bank within a few business days.

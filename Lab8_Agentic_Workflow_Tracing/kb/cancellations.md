# Cancellation Policy
Orders can be cancelled free of charge until the order is packed.

After the order is packed, a cancellation fee of Rs 20 applies.

Cancellation is not possible once the order is out for delivery, i.e. after the rider
has left the store with it. In that case you may refuse the delivery at the door, but
delivery fees are not refunded.

To cancel, open the Orders page and tap Cancel Order. Refunds for prepaid cancelled
orders are credited to the original payment method in 5-7 business days.

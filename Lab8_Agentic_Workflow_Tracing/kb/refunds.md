# Refund Policy
Perishable groceries: refund requests within 7 days of delivery.
Non-perishable items: within 30 days, unopened.
Refunds are credited to the original payment method in 5-7 business days.
Delivery fees are non-refundable once an order is out for delivery.

Perishable groceries include fresh fruit, vegetables, dairy such as milk and curd,
bread, eggs, meat and seafood. If a perishable item arrives spoiled, damaged or bruised,
raise a refund request from the Orders page with a photo of the item. Requests made
after the 7-day window cannot be accepted for perishable goods.

Non-perishable items such as packaged staples, rice, oils, bottles and household goods
can be returned within 30 days of delivery provided the item is unopened and in its
original packaging. Opened non-perishable items are not eligible unless they were
defective on arrival.

Once approved, the refund amount equals the price paid for the item. Refunds are
always credited to the original payment method; CartVista does not issue cash refunds.
